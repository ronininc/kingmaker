# Authorization to Release Information — TEMPLATE (distressed / pre-foreclosure)

> ⚠️ Skeleton, not legal advice — attorney/title review before use. This document is
> what lets you and the title company actually obtain the **loan payoff /
> reinstatement figure** and lien details from the seller's lender/servicer. Without
> it you cannot confirm the deal is solvent (payoff vs. your MAO), so on any
> pre-foreclosure / behind-on-payments / lien-encumbered deal, generate and get this
> signed EARLY — right after the PSA. Fill every `{{ }}`.

---

**AUTHORIZATION TO RELEASE INFORMATION**

Property: {{Property Address, City, State, ZIP}} — APN {{VERIFY}}
Owner(s) of record: {{Seller Name(s) — VERIFY}}
Loan / servicer (if known): {{Servicer name}} — Loan # {{VERIFY}}

I/we, the undersigned owner(s), authorize {{Servicer / lender / any lienholder / the
county / homeowners association}} to release to **{{Wholesaler / Company Name}}** and
to **{{Title Company / Escrow / Closing Attorney}}**, and to their authorized
representatives, all information regarding the above property and any loan or lien
secured by it, including but not limited to: current **payoff amount**,
**reinstatement quote**, loan balance and status, delinquency and default figures,
scheduled foreclosure/trustee-sale date, and the amount of any tax, HOA, mechanic's,
or judgment lien.

This authorization is for the purpose of a potential sale/closing of the property. A
photocopy or electronic copy of this authorization shall be as valid as the original.
This authorization remains in effect until {{date / the closing of the transaction}}.
