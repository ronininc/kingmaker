# Outreach Scripts (by motivation)

Personalize with REAL details only. `{{ }}` = fill from the lead row; never invent
a specific the source didn't give. Keep SMS opening short and non-salesy — the goal
is a reply, not a pitch. Always include an opt-out on SMS.

## SMS — first touch
**Generic / absentee landlord:**
> Hi {{Owner First}}, are you the owner of {{Property Address}}? I buy houses in the
> area as-is and may be interested. No pressure — reply STOP to opt out.

**Pre-foreclosure (soft, empathetic — never pushy):**
> Hi {{Owner First}}, I work with homeowners near {{City}} on options for {{Property
> Address}}, including a fast as-is sale. Happy to share what's possible if useful.
> Reply STOP to opt out.

**Probate / inherited:**
> Hi {{Owner First}}, I help families sell inherited homes like {{Property Address}}
> quickly and as-is, no repairs or cleanout needed. Would it help to talk options?
> Reply STOP to opt out.

## SMS — follow-up cadence (send only to non-opt-outs)
- +2 days: "Just circling back on {{Property Address}} — still open to an as-is offer?"
- +5 days: "No worries if the timing's off. If things change, I can close on your timeline."
- +10 days: "Last note from me on {{Property Address}} — here if you ever want a quick cash option."

## Cold call — opener + frame
> "Hi, is this {{Owner First}}? My name's ___ with ___ — I'll be quick. I buy homes
> in {{City}} as-is and came across {{Property Address}}. Have you thought at all
> about selling it, or is now not the right time?"

Then: condition → timeline → price expectation → book the walk-through. If not now,
ask permission to follow up and log the date.

## Direct mail — yellow letter (probate/absentee/tax-delinquent)
> Dear {{Owner Name}},
>
> I'm a local buyer interested in {{Property Address}}. I purchase homes as-is —
> no repairs, no agent fees, and I can close on your schedule. If you've considered
> selling, I'd welcome a short conversation. You can reach me at ___.
>
> Thank you, ___

## Rules
- No implication of being a licensed agent or the end all-cash buyer if untrue.
- Honor opt-outs instantly; scrub DNC before calls; SMS via a 10DLC-registered
  number. See `references/compliance-and-legal.md`.
- Follow-up is where deals close — most sellers convert after several touches, so
  always load the full cadence, not just the first message.
