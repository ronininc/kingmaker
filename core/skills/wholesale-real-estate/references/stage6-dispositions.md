# Stage 6 — Dispositions (find the cash buyer)

The stage teams underinvest in. A locked contract is worth nothing without a buyer
who closes. Build the buyer list as a compounding asset.

## Build a cash-buyer list free (public records)
Active investors reveal themselves in the recorder/assessor data:
1. Find **recent cash purchases** (deeds with no mortgage recorded) in the target
   submarket over the last 6–12 months — those buyers pay cash and buy again.
2. Find **absentee owners of multiple properties** (same mailing address across
   parcels) — portfolio landlords / flippers.
3. Capture buyer name + mailing address; skip trace (Stage 3 method) for contact.
4. Tag by property type/price they actually buy, so you match deals to the right
   buyer. Keep it in the CRM — it's reusable on every future deal.

## Paid marketplaces / accelerators
- **InvestorLift** — largest buyer network + blast engine ($6K–$15K+/yr).
- **Deal Run** (~$99/mo) — buyer ID + AI comps + repair estimates.
- **InvestorBase** (~$249/mo) — ranks likely cash buyers from public records.
- **REsimpli** ($199–$499/mo) — dispo built into the CRM.
- **Privy** — buyer/investor activity + comps.
- Plus **Facebook cash-buyer groups** and local REIA meetups (free).

## Deliverable
1. **Deal one-sheet** from `assets/deal-one-sheet.md`: address, ARV, repair
   estimate, asking (contract price + assignment fee), spread to buyer, photos
   (CLIENT-UPLOAD if not available — never fabricate), and the numbers that prove
   the margin.
2. **Buyer blast**: short email + SMS to the matched buyer list with the one-sheet
   and a clear CTA + deadline. Compliance same as Stage 4.
3. Log responses in the CRM; move `Status → Under Contract / Buyer Found`.

## Honesty
Only include photos/facts you actually have; mark the rest CLIENT-UPLOAD / VERIFY.
Overstated ARV or hidden repairs kill buyer trust and the assignment.
