# Compliance & Legal (read before Stages 4, 5, 7)

The skill produces work product. It does **not** give legal advice or sign off on
enforceability. Flag these to the team every time; they are where wholesalers get
sued or fined.

## Wholesaling legality & licensing (state-by-state, changing)
- Wholesaling is legal in most states **when done as assigning an equitable
  interest in a contract** — not brokering someone else's property without a
  license. The line matters.
- A growing number of states have passed or proposed laws that **restrict
  wholesaling or require a real-estate license** to market property. Some require
  disclosure that you hold a contract (equitable interest), not the property.
- **Action:** before operating in a market, confirm current state law and whether a
  license or specific disclosures are required, with local counsel. Do not assume
  a strategy legal in one state is legal in the next. Never state a jurisdiction's
  rule as settled without checking — cite that it must be verified.

## Contact law (Stages 3–4)
- **TCPA:** governs calls/texts to consumers. Violations carry per-message
  statutory damages — this is the single biggest litigation risk in outreach.
- **National DNC Registry:** scrub call and text lists before contact; honor
  opt-outs immediately and permanently.
- **10DLC:** SMS to US numbers must run through a registered brand/campaign or
  carriers filter/block it. The team registers this with their SMS provider.
- **State rules:** calling hours, state DNC lists, and consent requirements vary.

## Contracts & disclosure (Stages 5, 7)
- Use written PSA + Assignment; include the assignment clause and a due-diligence
  contingency as the exit.
- Disclose the assignment / fee where the contract or state requires. Non-disclosure
  is a common source of disputes and deal collapse.
- Earnest money, title, and lien clearance handled by a licensed title company /
  closing attorney. Distressed properties frequently carry tax, mechanic's, or HOA
  liens that must clear before close.

## Fair housing & honesty
- No steering, no discriminatory targeting or messaging.
- No implying licensure, direct-buyer status, or funds the team doesn't have.
- **No fabricated facts** anywhere — owner data, ARV, repair costs, photos. This is
  both an ethics rule and a fraud-exposure rule. Unverifiable = `Unknown` / `VERIFY`
  / `CLIENT-UPLOAD`.

The skill's job is to surface these and keep the work clean; the team's licensed
attorney and title partner make the final calls.
