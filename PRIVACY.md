# Kingmaker privacy notice

Plain words, short. The full rules for what Kingmaker may share are in [TERMS.md](TERMS.md).

## What stays on your Mac

Everything that is yours stays on your Mac: what you say and type, recordings and transcripts, files, screens, messages, contacts, accounts and keys, and your business records. Kingmaker runs its models on your Mac, and your keys stay in its vault on your Mac.

## What can leave, and when

- **Community learning (sharing).** It's on by default, and the first time Kingmaker has something to share it asks you. It sends only sanitized learning about how Kingmaker works, never your data. "sharing off" stops it, "what have you shared" shows the exact log, and "forget what I shared" asks for it to be erased. See TERMS.md for the full list. Until the collective's service is live, nothing is sent.
- **Cloud brains you choose.** If you pick Claude, ChatGPT or another cloud brain, what you ask it goes to that provider under their terms. The local model sends nothing out.
- **Updates.** Kingmaker checks Ronin's update server for new versions. The check carries this Mac's update key and the version it runs, nothing else.
- **Downloads it makes for you.** Models, tools and the knowledge library come from their publishers (for example Ollama, Hugging Face, GitHub and Tailscale), and those sites see an ordinary download request.
- **Your phone.** Kingmaker Phone talks to your Mac on your own network, or through your own Tailscale account if you set it up.
- **Paid add-ons**, if you have one, are checked against Ronin's update server so a refunded or revoked one stops working.

## The website

When you download Kingmaker from ronininc.org, the site counts the download: the date, the page you came from, your country and the kind of device. It stores no IP address and no full browser details, and it counts each visitor once a day without being able to follow you from day to day. The public count shows totals only.

## Who to ask

Book a time through ronininc.org. That is the only public way to reach Ronin Inc.
