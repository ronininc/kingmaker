# Government systems — elective exit and reduction inventory

Purpose: identify every government system, registry, account, and data-sharing
arrangement the subject appears in; classify whether the subject can actually
leave it, can only reduce exposure, or must remain while a status/activity
continues; and prepare the exit or reduction paperwork for the elective items.

This inventory is a discovery checklist, not a claim that every person is in
every system or that a named process will be available on the day it is checked.
Verify eligibility, consequences, current forms, and retention rules from the
agency's official source at run time.

## Classification

- **EXIT — voluntary:** the enrollment/account/registration is optional. The
  subject can withdraw, cancel, close, surrender, or let it lapse. Closing the
  account does not necessarily erase the government's historical record of the
  enrollment; record both facts separately.
- **REDUCE — record remains, exposure can be limited:** close the online access
  point, stop optional sharing, correct the record, freeze/lock access, choose
  paper/limited communications, surrender an unneeded credential, or disenroll
  prospectively. The underlying agency record remains under its retention law.
- **STAY — no elective exit while the trigger applies:** tax, court/probation,
  licensing while practicing, driving/registration while driving, and similar
  legal obligations. Do not sell these as opt-outs. The lawful options are
  compliance, correction, confidentiality where eligible, sealing/expungement
  where eligible, or ending the triggering activity/status properly.
- **UNKNOWN — discover first:** candidate systems where the report has not yet
  established whether Robbie is enrolled or what Arizona/federal rules allow.

- my Social Security and SSA online access.

**Class:** usually **EXIT/REDUCE**. Delete or close accounts that are no longer

payment/contact data where allowed, revoke connected applications, and request
closure in writing. Record that closing an access account does not delete tax,
benefit, military, court, or licensing records held behind it.

  affiliation through the county/state process if Robbie elects to do so. Check

  distributed. Ask separately what Arizona law makes public and whether a
  confidentiality protection applies.
- **Organ donor registration — EXIT:** remove the registration from Arizona's
  donor registry / Donate Life record and remove the donor designation at MVD if
  present, if that is Robbie's choice.
- **Selective Service — STAY for now:** registration information is not an
  ordinary voluntary marketing database. Robbie was born in 2002 and is still
  within the age band where the registration regime matters; his Marine service
  may already have satisfied or documented the requirement. Obtain a status
  information record if needed, but do not attempt to erase or evade registration

- **Jury-source lists — STAY/REDUCE:** a person generally cannot remove himself
  from the government's jury-eligibility universe in advance. Individual service
  is handled through summons, qualification, excuse, or deferral. Cancelling
  voter or driver records solely to avoid jury service has wider consequences
  and is not treated as a routine footprint opt-out.
- **Census — STAY:** decennial and survey responses required by law are not an
  elective subscription. Keep the case record limited to correcting agency
  contact/account artifacts where relevant.

## C. Travel, border, and transportation

- **Trusted Traveler Programs — EXIT:** TSA PreCheck, Global Entry, NEXUS, and
  SENTRI are voluntary. If enrolled and no longer wanted, use CBP/TSA's current
  withdrawal, non-renewal, or account-closure process and record whether the
  membership, Known Traveler Number, or historical application file remains.
- **Passport — REDUCE/STAY:** a passport is voluntary until international travel
  is chosen. It can expire or be surrendered through the State Department's
  process, but passport application records are retained. Do not surrender it as
  a footprint move without weighing identification and travel consequences.
- **REAL ID / driver credential — REDUCE/STAY while driving:** Robbie cannot
  delete the driver record and drive on it anyway. Options are limited to lawful
  credential choices, MVD privacy/sharing controls actually offered in Arizona,
  closing the online account, and correcting the record.
- **Vehicle title and registration — STAY while the vehicle is owned/driven:**
  end only through sale, transfer, non-operation, or the state's proper process.
  Public and lienholder copies may persist.
- **TSA screening databases encountered by flying — STAY if flying:** the
  practical election is not to fly. Secure Flight data for a booked flight is not
  a standing consumer opt-out.
- **FBI Voluntary Appeal File / UPIN — EXIT if he created one:** this is an
  opt-in file for firearm-transfer delays/denials. If present, ask FBI NICS for
  the current withdrawal process. Absence of a VAF cannot be inferred from no
  memory of one; discover first.
- **NICS/Federal firearm checks — STAY when purchasing through an FFL:** there
  is no advance personal opt-out from the check triggered by the purchase.

## D. Health and human-services systems

Discover enrollment before advising exit. Disenrollment can create coverage,
benefit, debt, or continuity consequences and must be a separate owner decision.

- **VA health and benefits accounts — UNKNOWN/REDUCE:** Robbie is a former
  Marine, but VA enrollment is not assumed. Inventory VA.gov, VA health care,
  disability, education, home-loan, and My HealtheVet artifacts if present.
  Online accounts can be closed/reduced; service and claims records cannot be
  deleted. Any disenrollment from VA health care or benefits requires a
  consequences review first.
- **Social Security retirement/disability and Medicare — REDUCE/STAY:** payroll
  participation while working is not elective. Benefit enrollment changes are
  window- and fact-specific; dropping premium-free Medicare Part A can affect
  Social Security benefits, so never file a disenrollment as a routine wipe.
- **Medicaid/AHCCCS, SNAP, cash assistance, unemployment, housing — EXIT/REDUCE
  if currently receiving:** a recipient can generally report closure or withdraw
  from a benefit going forward, but agency case, payment, integrity, and debt
  records remain. Closing can trigger repayment or loss of eligibility; inventory
  and consequences first. No enrollment is assumed.
- **Federal student aid/loans — REDUCE/STAY while a loan or aid record exists:**
  close unneeded online access and correct the record; loans and aid history do
  not disappear by account closure. If no aid history exists, an unused FSA ID
  account is a closure candidate after verification.
- **Arizona immunization information system (ASIIS) — UNKNOWN:** determine
  whether Robbie has a record and what Arizona's current opt-out, lock, or
  access limitation is. Do not promise deletion of provider-reported records.
- **Arizona health information exchange — UNKNOWN:** determine participation
  through treating providers and the current Arizona HIE opt-out process. This
  may limit future electronic sharing without erasing records already exchanged.
- **Arizona Controlled Substances Prescription Monitoring Program — REDUCE:**
  dispensing reports are provider/pharmacy driven, not a patient subscription.
  The remedy is obtaining and correcting his own report where the process allows,
  not elective removal.
- **State disease, trauma, cancer, and similar registries — REDUCE/STAY:**
  many are provider-mandated public-health registries. Seek access/correction;
  do not represent them as voluntary consumer lists.
- **NIH All of Us or another government research cohort — EXIT if enrolled:**
  research participation is voluntary and ordinarily has a withdrawal process;
  data already distributed in de-identified research may not be retractable.
- **Emergency notification systems (CodeRED/local alerts) — EXIT:** local alert
  profiles are generally voluntary. Delete the profile/account if one exists.
  Wireless Emergency Alerts are device settings, not a government enrollment;
  basic 911 location capability is not a removable consumer listing.
