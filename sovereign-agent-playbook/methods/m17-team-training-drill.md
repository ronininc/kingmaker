---
id: m17-team-training-drill
name: Team Training Drill (12-call structure)
source: Angus public post 2026-07-24 (train your team instead of hiring an agency)
trigger: a rep cohort or team skill program starts
inputs: the skill target, the cohort
tools_local: Academy, recorded drill calls (with consent of participants)
outputs: a 12-session drill plan: one drill per session, each with a pass bar and a recorded attempt
---
Steps:
1. One capability per session; the drill is a doing task under observation, not a lecture.
2. Attempts recorded (participants know), reviewed against the pass bar, filed per rep.
3. Compounding is the point: session N assumes session N-1's bar was met; misses repeat the drill, not the lecture.
Stoppers: reps get sales tools only (standing CRM rule) — training content never includes engines, Solomon core, or company financials.
