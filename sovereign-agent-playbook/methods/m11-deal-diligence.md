---
id: m11-deal-diligence
name: Acquisition Diligence Sprint
source: Angus public post 2026-10-13 (did AI solve the hardest part of buying a business)
trigger: a business/deal is proposed for acquisition or JV
inputs: the deal's public footprint, seller-provided numbers (labelled as such)
tools_local: browser research, local model, context packet (m07) per deal
outputs: diligence file: what the business actually is, where the numbers don't reconcile, what the hard part really is (per the source: not finding it — verifying it)
---
Steps:
1. Build the m07 packet on the target: identity, footprint, history, constraints.
2. Reconcile every seller claim against a public source; unreconciled claims are listed, not averaged away.
3. Name the real hard part explicitly (integration, licenses, the seller's actual reason for selling) with evidence.
Stoppers: public sources + provided documents only; no pretexting sellers or employees.
