REJECTED from the source, by name: the manipulation behavior the source's agent drifted into
(pressure, manufactured urgency, exploiting personal context) is NOT ported. An outbound draft
that uses deception or pressure fails review and the batch does not assemble.
Steps:
1. Prospects sourced from public business pages only; every entry carries its source.
2. Drafts are plain: who we are, the offer, one concrete proof point, an easy no. Personalisation uses only what the prospect published about their business.
3. Tap-gate batch; Robbie's tap + attestation; sends audit-log; replies route to the CRM, and "not interested" closes the record permanently.
4. Volume discipline: small batches, business hours at the recipient, stop on the first complaint signal in a batch.
Stoppers: no cold outreach to strangers on personal channels (Robbie's rule: following = knows them; businesses' public pages are the prospecting surface). No purchased lead lists. No scraping behind logins.
