---
id: m03-corpus-first-drafting
name: Corpus-First Drafting
source: Angus public post 2026-09-07 (notebook-based content process; writer persona fix)
trigger: any content draft (post, page, script)
inputs: the owner's own corpus (prior posts, transcripts, memos), a brief
tools_local: local model, corpus search over owner files
approval_gates: nothing publishes without the owner's tap (standing content rule)
outputs: draft + a source map (which corpus items fed which claims)
---
Steps:
1. Retrieve the owner's own material on the topic first; the draft may only assert what the corpus supports.
2. Draft in the owner's formats (locked templates where they exist).
3. Attach the source map; unsourced claims are cut or labelled analysis, never shipped silently.
Stoppers: none. Other people's private messages never enter the corpus.
