name: Call Archive Distillation
source: Angus public post 2026-07-17 (sales gurus made installable from recorded calls)
trigger: a recorded call archive (owner's own calls) is designated for method extraction
inputs: owner's own call recordings/transcripts
tools_local: local transcription, local model
outputs: distilled method cards: the move, the situation it fires in, the exact phrasing pattern, the outcome on the call
---
Steps:
1. Transcribe and segment the owner's own calls; index by situation (objection, close, discovery).
2. Extract reusable moves as method cards with the call + timestamp as the receipt.
3. Cards feed Academy training and the owner's own prep; they are internal methods, never published with client-identifying detail.
Stoppers: owner's own recordings only; other parties' private material never leaves the archive; no card ships with a client's name or business details attached.
