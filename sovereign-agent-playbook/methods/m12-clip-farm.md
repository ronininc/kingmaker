source: Angus public post 2026-09-15 (AI clip farm automation)
trigger: a long-form source (owner's own content) is approved for clipping
inputs: owner-owned source video/audio, the locked content formats
tools_local: transcription, local model for moment selection, the Clipper engine
outputs: clip candidates with moment receipts (timestamps + why), assembled per format spec
---
Steps:
1. Transcribe locally; select moments against the format spec (hook in first 3s, sourced claims only).
2. Every clip carries its moment receipt — no clip exists without the timestamp evidence behind it.
3. Posting follows the standing content rules: max cadence, proof before promotion, owner's tap.
Stoppers: owner's own content only — clipping third-party content for repost is refused (rights + platform rules). The source post's lesson stands: automation finds moments, taste decides; the tap is the taste gate.
