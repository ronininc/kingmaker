---
id: m16-pricing-intel
name: Pricing Intelligence (public data)
source: Angus public post 2026-07-28 (beating a pricing algorithm with public data + skills)
trigger: a pricing decision for an offer, a listing, or a market entry
inputs: public price surfaces for the market in question
tools_local: browser research, local model
outputs: pricing file: the public data the market actually runs on, where our price sits, the move
---
Steps:
1. Identify the cheap public data the incumbents' pricing runs on; collect it openly.
2. Map our offer against the real distribution, not the advertised one.
3. Recommend with the data attached; a price change is Robbie's tap like any money move.
Stoppers: public data only; no scraping behind logins, no fake accounts, no ToS-breaching collection — if the data needs one of those, the method reports blocked-with-reason.
