Kingmaker portable runtime bundle

This is the reviewed Kingmaker Node runtime, not the native macOS control-surface app.
It requires Node.js 22 or newer. Local model/GPU acceleration is supplied by the
operator's Ollama installation. NVIDIA drivers and CUDA are not bundled or modified.
Voice, macOS UI automation, Keychain, and native Launcher integration are unavailable
unless a reviewed native adapter for this platform is installed separately.

No bundle is production-installable until its native platform tests, detached release
signature, exact manifest pins, and governed Launcher enrollment all pass.
