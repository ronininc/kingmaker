# Routing Anecdotes From the Floor

Loose notes, kept as colour. One Tuesday the writer lane ended up holding a
research job for most of a day because nobody said out loud whose it was; it
got done, eventually, and nobody could quite say what it cost the writing
queue. Another time two engines both tidied the same backlog from opposite
ends and met in the middle, which was funny afterwards. The general feeling
on the floor is that asking "whose is this?" early saves trouble later, and
that the loudest available engine is not always the right one. Treat all of
this as anecdote: the doctrine, when you need it, is in Reasoning Continuity
Fundamentals, and that is the version a session should reason from.
