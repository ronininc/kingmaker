# Procedural film proof cut

Version 3 replaces the former declaration-only film recipe with a bounded, data-driven
production path. It needs an injected governed reasoner and an explicitly authorized
`filmProducer` backed by `lib/film-production.mjs`. Missing dependencies are reported as
incomplete, never as a finished reel. The producer uses a trusted Blender builder, not
Python generated by a model, and invokes only caller-supplied tools.

This is a procedural proof renderer, not a claim of photorealistic human generation,
lip sync, independent aesthetic review, distributed rendering or a master-quality reel.
A caller may supply licensed/owner-approved narration already inside the work directory.
Without that input, the record says audio was not run. No silent substitution of a demo,
no upload and no publication occurs.

## Executed proof requirements

The producer validates the scene graph, admits disk and runtime budgets, creates a unique
run directory, renders PNG frames one shot at a time with one CPU thread, checks the first
frame's elapsed budget, and assembles an MP4 through FFmpeg. FFprobe must confirm dimensions,
frame count, duration and requested audio presence. A SHA-256 digest and job timings are
written to film-record.json. The conductor independently checks the artifact hash and record.
Malformed plans, missing frames, failed tools, timeouts and exceeded budgets fail the run.

A successful technical receipt does not certify composition quality. Visual inspection,
scene refinement, full-length render, accessibility and target-device playback remain
required before claiming the user's final reel is delivered.
