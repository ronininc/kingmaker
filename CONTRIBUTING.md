Ronin Inc. sells commercial licences for Kingmaker, so it has to be able to license every line of it, including yours.

By submitting a contribution (a pull request, a patch, or any code, documentation or data you send for inclusion), you:

1. confirm you wrote it or otherwise have the right to submit it, including your employer's permission if your employer has rights in what you make;
2. grant Ronin Inc. a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, copy, modify, distribute, sublicense and relicense it, including under commercial terms;
3. grant Ronin Inc. and everyone who receives Kingmaker a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable licence under any patents you can license that your contribution would otherwise infringe, to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import and otherwise transfer it as part of Kingmaker;
4. provide it as is, without warranty of any kind; and
5. agree that everyone else receives it under [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`) to record that you agree to the above.

You keep your own copyright. If you can't agree to this, please don't submit the contribution.

Kingmaker's engine source is not in this repo, so changes to the engine can't be accepted here; report what you found in an issue instead.
